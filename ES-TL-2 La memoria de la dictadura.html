Marking principles applied

MARKING PRINCIPLES — translation into Spanish
Accents: non-grammatical accent errors are tolerated (e.g. jovenes for jóvenes) unless they cause ambiguity — entro for entró, esta for está, si for sí, mas for más and el for él are not tolerated.
Spelling: non-grammatical misspellings are tolerated as long as they are not ambiguous and are not in the wrong language.
Verb endings must be correct and are not treated as spelling errors.
Adjective endings must be correct and are not treated as spelling errors.
Repeated capitalisation errors are not penalised (e.g. Español for español).
Any appropriate alternative that does not appear in the Acceptable Answers column is credited.
How this page marks. It checks that every element of an acceptable answer is present — in any order, allowing synonyms, tolerated accent errors and minor stem misspellings — and withholds the mark when the Reject column applies or a required element is missing. The real mark scheme also credits any appropriate alternative a human examiner judges correct, and no program can reproduce that. So where you disagree, read the mark scheme for that chunk and award yourself the mark with a justification. Your download shows both totals and your reasoning, and your teacher decides.
